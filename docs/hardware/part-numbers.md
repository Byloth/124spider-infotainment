# Part numbers by market

This page lists what to order for each market: the hub, the cable set, and the kit number where one
exists. Every market is listed. If you have chosen a market elsewhere on the site, the full table below
narrows to yours and says so. Without JavaScript it always shows every market.

## The short version

| Market | Hub | Cable set | Kit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **North America** | `TK78-66-9U0C` (now `-9U0E`) | `C922-V6-605A` | `0000-8F-Z34`: hub + cable set |
| **Europe and UK** | `TK78-66-9U0C` (now `-9U0E`) | `C830-V6-60Z` | No single number. Order the lines separately |
| **ADR** (Australia, NZ, Asia-Pacific) | `TK78-66-9U0C` (now `-9U0E`) | `C924-V6-605` | No single number |
| **Japan** | `TK78-66-9U0C` (now `-9U0E`) | `C921-V6-605` | No single number |

The hub is the **same part worldwide**<SourceCite ids="E-06,E-10" />. Only the cable set's number
differs, and reports say **any market's cable set fits any car**. They differ by catalogue entry, not by
design<SourceCite ids="E-10,E-19" />. Order the one your local parts counter can supply.

## Everything, with dated prices

<PartFinder />

Every price above is an **observation with a date**, some from 2018, gathered from dealer listings and
owners' posts. None of them is a quote. Prices for the North American kit have gone from $199 at launch
to about $250 today<SourceCite ids="E-09,E-21" />.

## The hub

- **`TK78-66-9U0C`** is the part every guide names. Genuine units carry an **orange label** and "Made in
  Japan"<SourceCite ids="E-06,E-10,E-01" />.
- It has been superseded, first by **`-9U0D`** and then by **`-9U0E`**, which US catalogues list as
  replacing the B, C and D revisions<SourceCite ids="E-21,E-22" />. A parts counter will likely offer you
  the 9U0E today.
- **Do not buy the earlier revisions.** Plain `TK78-66-9U0`, `-9U0A` and `-9U0B`, and the green-label
  China-market **`KD5J-66-9U0`**, are reported not to work with the CarPlay firmware<SourceCite ids="E-06,E-10,E-01" />.
- The hub that comes **out** of a 124 is **`N243-66-9U0A`** or **`-9U0B`**. Keep it<SourceCite ids="E-01" />.

❓ **Nobody has reported fitting the current `-9U0E` to a 124.** Mazda's catalogues say it replaces the
9U0C, and the owners in the reports fitted a 9U0C, or a 9U0D on Mazdas<SourceCite ids="E-17" />. It
should behave the same, but that is the catalogue's word, not an owner's.

## The cable set

Each set holds two cables: **grey/blue to a brown plug**, and **grey/green to a black plug**. It also
includes nine cable ties, sponge tape and a supplement to the owner's manual<SourceCite ids="E-08" />.
The North American number moved from `C922-V6-605` to **`C922-V6-605A`**<SourceCite ids="E-21" />.

## Ordering it

- **North America** has the only single kit number, **`0000-8F-Z34`**: one hub and one cable set. Mazda's
  dealer bulletin allows **1.5 hours** of labour for fitting it<SourceCite ids="E-09" />.
- **Europe and the UK** order separate lines: the hub, the cable set `C830-V6-60Z`, the sponge-tape set
  **`C830-V6-693`**, and the manual supplement **`4100-77-300EN`** (other languages exist, such as `-ES`)<SourceCite ids="E-27,E-24" />.
- **Australia, New Zealand and Japan** have no kit number either. Order the hub and your market's cable
  set.

## Where to buy genuine

- **Any Mazda dealer's parts counter**, by part number.
- **Dealer-run online shops.** US dealers sell the kit on their own parts sites and on eBay and
  Amazon<SourceCite ids="E-21,E-19" />. The Amazon listing that 68wooley and Ameridan bought from was
  sold as Mazda's own kit, and its reviews describe genuine parts<SourceCite ids="E-34a,A-01" />.
- **UK dealer web shops** list the parts by number<SourceCite ids="E-24,E-25a" />. In Germany, OEM parts
  sites do the same<SourceCite ids="E-26" />.

Anything much cheaper than these is likely a copy, even when the listing uses Mazda's part number in its
title. [Genuine vs clone](/hardware/oem-vs-clone) sets out what the reports say about those.
