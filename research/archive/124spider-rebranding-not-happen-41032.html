So the firmware is now 70.00.100A-NA.

But he told me that stage three of the firmware upgrade, the change of branding from Mazda to Abarth simply would not work.

Then he installed the new usb hub.

Most of it works fine, through the iPhone connected by cable and CarPlay running. A few things don’t work. Auto locking especially does not work so I am going to have to teach myself to lock it each time I park it.

I have tried to do the stage 3 brand change myself following the links through Ameridan thatI found on the forum.

I put the drive the top USB port, waited 15 minutes, but nothing happened. It just seems to stick and do nothing at all. Tried several times, ensured phone had been unpaired and iPhone’s Bluetooth was turned off. Audio source was FM per Ameridan’s instructions.

But still nothing happens.

I can actually live with the Mazda branding, as all I was really looking for was a good navigation system and I can use Waze through my phone. Also able to listen to my podcasts or the music from my phone perfectly OK. And the phone thru CarPlay works fine.

But it would be nice to have the Abarth symbol show up.

I wonder too whether would give full functionality on things like automatic locking and Bluetooth if the branding is right.

By the way, I also purchased wireless phone charger kit from 124 Spider, which has also been installed and worked perfectly well.

However as Bluetooth does not seem to work properly and I have to link the iPhone by cable to get CarPlay to work the wireless charger is superfluous.

Anybody got any suggestions?

Given that you can’t install tweaks (rebranding or otherwise) through the USB port, I’m guessing that either the id7 patch was not included in your upgrade kit or else it was and the shop who installed your upgrade did not install it. I would ask the seller if the id7 patch was included with the kit. As Chris said, the id7 patch needs to be installed before upgrading the system software. The only way to restore USB tweak access if id7 was not installed before the system software was upgraded is to do the serial hack. The serial access hack instructions can be found here. I’m not familiar with the wireless charging kit, but the system software update should not have affected the Bluetooth or door locking issues you’re having. Don’t know why you have those problems. More questions for the fellow who sold you the kit.
