I installed the HMYC 2.1 version with wired Carplay bought on Amazon. Works perfectly. I'm very thankful to Ash8 on this forum for the firmware file and all the resources he has posted. The instructions that come with the kit are woefully inadequate. It's basically just a wiring diagram. I devised my own minimally invasive procedure after my research on this site and watching a few YouTube videos. It was surprisingly quick and easy, with no need to dismantle the centre console. It's a significant upgrade to the car and I'm so happy I did it.. I will report back if the unit fails.
