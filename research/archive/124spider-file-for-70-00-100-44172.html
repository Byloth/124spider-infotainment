I believe you will need the Oceania files. They’ll be denoted v70.00.100 4A N. Do not use the ones marked NA N (for North America,) EU N (for Europe) or the ones for Japan.

That said, ever since Mazda cracked down on distribution of the files around two years ago, they’ve been tough to find. You might want to ask over at the Mazda3Revolution forums and a user named ASH8. They may have a lead on the files you need. Good luck!

If you don’t already have them, here are the full installation instructions: AA/CarPlay Installation instructions
