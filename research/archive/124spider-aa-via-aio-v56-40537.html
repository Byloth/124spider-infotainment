I just updated to v70 firmware and the higher power USB hub and have AndroidAuto running. AA is an option with the older customised AIO from ameridan/68wooley so you might want to try that first.

I've had a play with the latest AIO and the choices are many and a little bewildering. The main reason as far as I can tell for upgrading the USB hub is to get higher power so that your phone actually charges at the same time as running Navigation on Google Maps or Waze and playing music etc. whereas with the original hub your phone will just keep discharging with all that running. The upgraded hub just has the extra power and is not specific for Carplay.

Like you I was going to try AA on v56 but I had bought the upgraded hub so was kind ocommitted to go to v70.
