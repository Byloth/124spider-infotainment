Touchscreen enable tweaks do not work with official mazda AA only carplay and normal mazda interface. If you're using Android devices (and wanted to use touchscreen for Android auto) i would suggest install AA Tru_go_v1.15b ---->>> AA Tru_go V1.15 FW59.00.545-FW70.00.xx-Fw70.00.352

Speedometer & video player (will run) but do not work well all the time, mostly lagging on Fw70 due to memory/diskspace issues because the official AA/CP uses alot of it. FW59 (no AA/CP) do not have this problem.

@Tristan-cx5
Just asking: Isn't any AA tweak like yours interfering with installed official AA/CP?
Click to expand...
official AA and unofficial share similar files/codes/resources (audio, video focus, navigation) therefore only one can be enabled. When one is disabled all memory allowances will be dedicated to that particular AA app and completely shutdown the other without issues.
