Yup, the cards are VIN locked, so swapping them between vehicles is a definite no-go. You can likely imagine a big part of that reasoning is to prevent fraudulent card copying. And as noted by some others, it appears that the folks that were selling the knock-offs on eBay are no longer in business as somehow Naviextras has found a way to identify them and ensure they can never be updated. My rampant speculation is that the cards have the equivalent of a serial number buried someone in their content and now Naviextras checks to see if more than one VIN is attached to a given serial number. If they find more than one, they all get banned as fraudulent.

So, back to the original question you posed at the top of the thread. Need a card? Visit your dealer and be prepared to pay the piper. Or, as others have noted, you can spend the same amount of money and replace the guts of your infotainment system and use your phone for navigation... and coping with the reliability of AA and ACP to display navigation content on your screen/ If you have other reasons for installing AA or ACP, then the head unit swap might be the route to go. I have AA in other vehicles and I'm not a fan. Others like it. So depending upon your personal likes and dislikes it could be a valid solution for you if you don't want to buy an OEM nav card for your newer Spider.
