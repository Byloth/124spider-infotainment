Sammy124 said:
Hmm.. I'm thinking I'll suffer with the possibility of echo on BT for now. What's the ramifications if the CMU gets bricked? will the dealer install a new one under warranty?
Click to expand...
If that happened, you'd probably want to put the original hub back in before you took it to the dealer. If they see the updated hub, they'll probably figure out you modified the system. It's pretty obvious if you're looking, with that little phone icon by the top USB port. With the original installed you could always plead ignorance: "Hey, I started the car and it just wouldn't turn on...I don't know what's wrong." > Or you could just take a chance that they wouldn't see the new hub. Also, if they did replace the CMU, it'll most likely come back with v59 installed. That means you'd need to hack the CMU via the serial port before you could install the id7 tweak to use USB.

And just BTW...apparently the echo issue is pretty much Mazda-specific, from what I see on their forums.
