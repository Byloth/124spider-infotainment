Overview

Mazda Radio Replacement

Upgrade your Mazda today to enable Apple CarPlay® & Android Auto® in less than an hour! Infotainment.com has taken the initiative to develop an easy DIY upgrade. Simply update the Mazda Connect radio software and install a replacement USB media hub (with new cables). That's it! The factory update is not available for individual sale, as it is exclusively sold as part of the complete upgrade kit.   Note - If your radio’s software version is lower than 30.XX.XX, please see our Installation Tab above and follow Step B.

Mazda Connect® 

To enjoy the benefits of Apple CarPlay® and Android Auto® with your Mazda, follow these simple steps if your vehicle's radio software is version 29.99.XXX or lower.

  1. Download the 31.00.100A update from this link.
  2. Unzip the downloaded file on your Windows PC.
  3. Prepare a USB Drive:
  • Ensure it’s at least 2GB and formatted to FAT32.
  • Copy the file named "cmu140_NA_31.00.100A_reinstall.up" to the root of your USB drive.
  1. Follow the Installation Video steps above to complete the software update process.
