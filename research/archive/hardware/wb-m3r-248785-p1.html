I contacted my local Mazda Dealer and they stated that for my car (2018 Mazda 3) the latest Android Auto part numbers were as follows:
TK78669U0E - My unit lists Ver P3 (Made in Japan)
410077300 EN (manual)
C830V6693 (foam tape)
C830V660Z (USB cables / cable ties)

Dealer price was £232.76 but I obviously would have had to collect it too. I found the Tyneside Automotive website by pure chance and ordered all parts individually for £193 delivered. It was a "special order" from the Mazda UK warehouse and arrived in four business days.


Just leaving this here in case others are struggling to find genuine parts outside of the dealership.
