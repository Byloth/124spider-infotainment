frank - I don't know what you mean by Abarth simulation. Do you mean the animated exit? or the animated boot?

bob - It is starting to sound like the Mazda uninstall routine is not fully debugged. It is actually not an uninstall, but a install "back to factory", which may in fact be erroneous. Or it could be your prior uninstall of the DOS based tweaks left something behind??

I can't speak from experience as I'm still on my original version 56 firmware. Reboot CMU = Press & Hold: Nav + Mute for 10 seconds.
If that doesn’t result in the desired behavior, you can also try a RESET:

  1. Select SETTINGS from the Home screen.
  2. Select SYSTEM
  3. Select Restore All Factory Settings to reset system to original settings, press Yes to confirm.
