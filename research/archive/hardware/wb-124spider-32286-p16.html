I have just finished installing Apple CarPlay, there was a problem with some of the software and I struggled until @68wooley and @bradley came to my rescue...literally. I also want to thank @ameridan for the comprehensive brief. The software issue was of my own doing, I hadn’t checked the ID7 files when I copied them to the usb, there was a component missing. Note to self, don’t assume anything. So I had to do the more complicated serial hack, thank goodness I had some help, that was a challenge mentally. Note to self, two heads are better than one (particularly if one of them has some idea what he is doing!). In terms of the strip down, it’s straightforward, but don’t rush. Some of the noises as things ‘clip out’ are wrenching, but it was fine, just take care not to catch any of the plastics as they are soft and prone to scratches. I used Alie-express for my USB hub, it was $103 all in and perfect, though I had to buy some fabric tape. The wiring is straightforward. Putting things back together is just a ‘caffuffle’ (technical word), the CMU takes some getting in and you always feel like you might be trapping a wire or something, but you aren’t, it is just fiddly. Some of the trim line up takes some messing with, but it all goes back fine. Oh, and Apple CarPlay, it’s brilliant. Thanks again @68wooley and @bradley.
