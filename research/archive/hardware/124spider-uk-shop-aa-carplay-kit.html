Fiat never offered Android Auto or CarPlay functionality for the 124 Spider, but using original equipment parts and software, it is now possible to add this functionality to all Fiat & Abart 124 Spiders which have the 7 inch infotainment display.

This kit provides full OEM Android Auto and Apple CarPlay functionality to your car, retaining all Fiat or Abarth branding, logos and text, and ensuring that the OEM Navigation system remains functional. OEM Android Auto and Apple CarPlay is a wired system, meaning you need to connect the phone to the car using an USB cable. No official OEM wireless system was ever developed for these cars, and any such examples seen on some websites are fakes (various 3rd party dongles may work to make the OEM system work in a similar way to a true wireless system, but we do not currently recommend any such dongles).

This installation of this kit  requires various software updates to the car's entertainment system as well as hardware installation in order to function properly. It is quite easy for the uninitiated to damage the car's CMU (entertainment system) during that process if performed incorrectly. It is for this reason that we have produced an exclusive set of instructions which we ship with every Android Auto & Apple CarPlay kit, detailing exactly how to perform the upgrades to your car's systems, whilst we also provide all the required software and hardware for you to perform the upgrade.*

However, we realise that some customers will still be uneasy performing these steps themselves, so we also offer a full fitting service - we will do the physical installation work, and will update all the necessary software to the latest stable version, ensuring that all Fiat / Abarth tools and functionality remain and work as expected following the Android Auto / CarPlay installation. We fully test the installation to ensure full functionality before you drive away.

When fitted by 124 Spider UK at our South London premises, we can update your system to Android Auto / Apple CarPlay regardless** of the original firmware version on your car, and will also update the Gracenote music database on your car - this upgrade is not available from any other supplier (and due to software re-distibution laws can not be included in our self-fit kits).

We also have the fastest fitting service in the UK, being able to complete this retrofit in under 2 hours - some other suppliers quote 3 or 4 hours, and so try to charge you more than is necessary. That is not our philosophy!
 
If you wish to order our fitting service, select the "Fitted by 124 Spider UK" option above and pay for the order. We will then contact you via email to arrange a fitting date and time. No fitting slots will be offered until we have received a fully paid order.

(please ensure your existing entertainment system firmware is LOWER than version 59.00.021 if you intend to fit this kit yourself)

* it is illegal to distribute or download original Mazda firmware files (software) from the internet. As such, we will only supply the software with our complete kit.
** cars with firmware version 74.00.010 and above can not be updated
