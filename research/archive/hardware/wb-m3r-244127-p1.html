Do check that cable. Two weeks after my AA/CarPlay upgrade my Nav quit working. Turned out that the GPS antenna connector was not firmly seated in the socket and had worked itself loose. A good way to check is in the info screen in the Nav. See how your GPS connection looks. That was my clue…I was getting no usable satellites. Now after resetting the GPS antenna connector correctly all is well.

I thought the problem was with the SD card but I actually just needed to upgrade to a genuine USB hub. I originally bought TK78-66-9U0C from eBay and replaced it with TK78-66-9U0D.
