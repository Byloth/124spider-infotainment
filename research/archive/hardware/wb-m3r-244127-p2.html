Stumbled across this thread as I had the same problem after the installation of a new USB hub from alibaba. I noticed that the SD card lock switch was always on "locked" when I removed the card. Turns out the the slot is narrower than the original one. I taped the lock switch to stay in the unlocked position and everything works now. Obviously the MZD needs to be able to write on the SD Card.
