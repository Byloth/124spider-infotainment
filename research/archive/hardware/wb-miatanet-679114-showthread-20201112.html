GENUINE MAZDA Car Play plus Android Auto Install Instructions.

For ND MX-5/Miata OWNERS Worldwide,
ALL of these listed parts have been installed and work in many ND’s Worldwide, see the Video’s in Links.
Also installed, tested and working in Mazda 2, Mazda 3, Mazda 6, CX-3, 5, 8, 9 and MX-5 around the world,
all only Genuine Mazda Parts/Software/Manuals.

THIS DETAIL IS ONLY FOR MAZDA'S WITH ''MZD/MAZDA CONNECT'' INFOTAINMENT SYSTEM..

Mazda Motors Europe (MME) has released Car Play and Android Auto, from Aug 6th 2018, genuine from your Mazda Dealer,
220 Euros for Parts only and 360 Euro for parts and Installation at a Mazda Dealer.

Mazda North American Operations (MNAO) has also released the Genuine Parts Listed Below for the 2018 Mazda 6
(above Sport trim) for their Customer Comfort ‘Free’ update program. While you may find the Genuine Parts very difficult
to find supply as they are now restricting to Mazda 6 ONLY and via a VIN Number must also be supplied.
Yes, you will find some on-line USA, Canada and Europe Mazda Dealers who are prepared to sell the OEM Parts needed.

Apparently December, 2018 this year is the month when CP/AA is released for all models and Parts stocks will be freely available…?
In the meantime there are some on-line Mazda Dealers who will sell and or take orders and you then wait in a waiting line.
Mazmart.com is a good one to order through, they get some parts direct from Japan sources also at a limited number at a time and
They will ship Internationally.

MNAO and ‘some’ of their US Mazda Dealers are saying the OEM Parts Numbers are ONLY for the 2018 Mazda 6,
I can assure this is not correct.
Europe Only lists one set of part numbers for ALL Mazda Models with MZD CONNECT.

The 2019 Mazda USA Parts EPC is also recently out and showing genuine Mazda CP/AA with the exact same main Parts as listed Below.
The MUST have all new AUX HUB is the same Mazda Parts number worldwide for ALL Mazda Models and the all new USB Cable Set
is also the same for all models, USB Cable Sets are SPECIFIC for each Country and Region by Part Number,
Plus the end USB Plugs at all ends are identical worldwide.
Have confirmed a MNAO C922 Cable Set will fit and work on a Europe Spec Mazda and the Euro EU N Spec C830 USB Cable Set
will Install and work on any NA N Specification/Sold Mazda.
Plus the all new AUX Hub Modules are ALL the same anyway.
Only REAL Difference is the FIRMWARE/Software which is Country and Region Specific as it always been the case.

Here are the Genuine MAZDA Part Numbers for the 3 main Regions of the World.

QUANTITY is ONE OF EACH ONLY.

3. TK78-66-9U0C New AUX Unit, MUST be Installed LAST.

FIRMWARE/SOFTWARE INSTALLATION- First:
There is a new 2018 CMU Firmware update procedure for Worldwide, see Pdf attachment in link above,
yes it is from Europe but it is the same for North America and there are a few important procedural changes that MUST be Performed...

CP/AA Installation.
Do this project all at the same time, Mazda allows about 1.5 hours.
And do not start engine once you start this Firmware update process.
Yes can drive car once Firmware is successfully installed,
and BEFORE New Parts are installed LAST.

FIRST..
Must First install new 70's FIRMWARE, use new PDF Instructions.
Run and use the Software for a few Minutes before shutting car down again so YOU can confirm your 70’s Firmware WORKS OK,
YOU WILL NOT SEE CP/AA as a working Option UNTIL YOU then Renew the New AUX Hub and 2 New USB Cables.
Turn OFF car/ACC mode once done (Two Quick ONE after the Other Presses of S/S Button)
DO NOT have Feet ON Brake or Clutch Pedals, DO NOT Have Engine IGNITION ON or ENGINE RUNNING!

Then start pulling apart your dash/console, you need to access CMU and AUX Hub unit.
SEE THE VIDEOs IN THE SERVER LINK ABOVE.
Remove CMU (so you can install new USB cables.)
Remove OLD AUX Hub Unit and discard.
UNPLUG old USB Cable Plug and TAPE Back as it is NEVER re-used.

Position, Lay and Fix the Two New USB Cables and connect to NEW AUX Hub and connect both to your CMU.
Re-install new AUX Hub and CMU.
Verify and check.

Now Push S/S Button ONCE to put system in ACC mode, the new Firmware should initialize and start....you now have a working CP/AA.

PUSH CLICK BACK IN ALL REMOVED DASH TRIM PARTS, AND SCREWS.
MAKE SURE ALL WIRING IS CONNECTED AND NOT PINCHED ANYWHERE
AS IT COULD BE CUT ON ANY SHARP DASHBOARD METAL BRACKETS.

REPEAT Warnings:
One MUST install/run the new 70's Firmware BEFORE you install Two all new USB Cables and all New AUX Hub Module, in that order.
GENUINE CP/AA will not work with trying to install ‘short cuts’ or trying to fool the system, you cannot.

Mazda WARRANTY:
Mazda Japan stipulates that this CP/AA Retrofit Upgrade IS NOT to be used by any Mazda Dealer as a Repair Method Procedure in the event of CMU
or old AUX (original) becomes defective in a Customers car and needs renewing or repairing.
Firmware 59.00.xxxx will still be used as an update to cars without CP/AA, until further notice.
Mazda stipulates NOT TO Update with 70's Firmware UNLESS Owner is to have new AUX Unit Upgrade.
70's Firmware does not over-ride 59 Firmware use at THIS STAGE and must not be installed unless new CP/AA is to be fitted.
Install of these Genuine Mazda Parts will not Void any part of your New Car Warranty in any Country.
