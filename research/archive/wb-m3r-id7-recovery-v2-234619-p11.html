I just put something together using Mazda wiring diagrams. You will need at a minimum:
2 amp 12VDC power supply
CMU & Display
Multi-function commander control
AUX Hub Module original
AUX Hub Module (CP/AA version) & cables
USB serial module to connect for installing ID7 etc.

All this will get you is a basic CMU that will boot and allow you to up/down grade firmware and install hacks. Since there are no other car modules on the CANBUS (ECU/TAU/Etc.) not much will work or even display - radio, speed/temperature signals, NAV etc. The AIO speedometer will display but all the signals will be default without live CANBUS data.

You can order these from a number of places - ebay, Chinese markets, auto parts houses or salvage them from junk cars. Getting them from a junk yard also lets you salvage the cables and connectors.

I just used jumpers to connect all mine as I do a lot of electronics hobby work. Not having the car harnesses and original hub USB cable makes it a bit harder and requires some familiarity reading Mazda wiring diagrams and connector signal understanding. There are not many power or signal wires between the CMU/Hub/Commander.
