To get the latest genuine Mazda MZD Connect firmware (v74.00.324A) for your Mazda 2, Mazda 3, Mazda 6, CX-3, CX-5, CX-9, or MX-5 (all vehicles using the MZD Connect infotainment system, typically 2014 – 2024 models):

📨 Step 1 – To register interest in the firmware upgrade package
  • Post a photo of your current firmware version screen in this thread which is monitored by @ASH8 :
  • https://www.mazda3revolution.com/th...om/threads/picture-posting-only-this-works.255827/?post_id=2590524#post-2590524
  • (Settings → System → About → Version Information)
🔸 @ASH8 is based in Australia – please allow up to 18 hours for a reply.

🧩 Step 2 – Two-step upgrade path (if you’re below v70)

If your current firmware is below 70.00.021A, you must perform a two-step update:

  1. Upgrade first to an intermediate v59.xx or v70.xx build (ASH8 will advise which one).
  2. Then proceed to v74.00.324A.
    This staged process ensures the CMU’s bootloader and file-system are updated safely and avoids “bricking” the unit.

🧰 Step 3 – CarPlay / Android Auto hardware requirements

Firmware v70 and later enable CarPlay and Android Auto, but hardware support depends on your USB hub:

RequirementDescription
Wired CarPlay / Android AutoNeeds the updated dual-port USB hub (e.g. TK78-66-9U0E) and matching cables (C922-V6-605A kit).
Wireless CarPlay / Android AutoThe OEM Mazda hub supports wired only. To go wireless, use a 3rd-party adapter (Carlinkit / Ottocast U2-Air) or an aftermarket CMU/head-unit upgrade that includes Wi-Fi + Bluetooth capability.
Older single-port hubsWill not support CarPlay/Android Auto even after the firmware update.

If you’re unsure which hub you have, look for part numbers such as TK78-66-9U0C/D/E on the underside of the USB module.

🧾 Firmware details & official thread

📄 Full instructions and downloads:
🔗 Firmware Help & Request Guide (ASH8)

⚠ Important:
Only use firmware links provided directly by @ASH8.
Files from YouTube, eBay, or other unofficial sources are often corrupted or incomplete and can brick your CMU.

📦 Final version summary

  • Latest & final official firmware: v74.00.324A
  • Adds: Apple CarPlay™ & Android Auto™ (wired)
  • Applies to: All MZD Connect vehicles (2014 – 2024)
  • Replaced by: New .kwi-based “Mazda Connect Gen 7” system on 2019-onward models (not compatible with this update).
