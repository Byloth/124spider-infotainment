The longest part of the exhaust install is getting the tips sitting equal in the bumper cutouts. Due to the force put on the AxleBack by the flange, you may need to spend some time tweaking to get everything straight. This entirely depends on your car and exhaust and how much you care.

The Ragazzon unit I had had adjustable everything, whereas many non silenced AxleBacks are solid pieces

But yes, the actual install is easy!

I think you'll eventually determine that the Speedo tweak really doesn't work very reliably, most particularly the compass part. The rest seems to be fine, and I did like the right hand detailed info panel. But ulitmately, I uninstalled it and put in the Mazda Speedo tweak version, and it too I have now removed.

One tweak I will highly recommend, especially if you use the nav system and do any amount of Interstate or freeway driving.... is the ability to operate the touchscreen while the vehicle is in motion. With the nav on, as you drive along a major highway, there will be a right hand side column appear which lists the upcoming exits in order of proximity, along with what services are available if you take them. So if you need to do a fuel stop, you can tap on the exit you wish to take, click on the service station you want to use, and add it as an intermediary waypoint. Quick. Easy. Super useful.

Congratulations on your efforts to date, but be warned... modding can be addictive...
