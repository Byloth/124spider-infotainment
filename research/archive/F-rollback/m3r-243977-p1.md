## [Tristan-cx5] 2021-09-06 (post 2519853)
1. Yes, technically you can. It does not brick the CMU (https://www.amazon.com/s?k=cmu+Mazda3&tag=vs-pl-mazda3revolution-com-20&rel=nofollow), if you do it properly/according to the instructions. But if you do it wrong, you could brick your CMU (https://www.amazon.com/s?k=cmu+Mazda3&tag=vs-pl-mazda3revolution-com-20&rel=nofollow). But recently nearly all links and servers with inofficial firmware files were deleted by the copyright owners. They do not want to see the files in the wild anymore, which is their legal right. Mazda (https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Mazda&tag=vs-pl-mazda3revolution-com-20&rel=nofollow) intends to let the firmware update only be done by the dealers/workshops.
2. Only updating the firmware to EU does not change the frequencies, which can be received, as they are stored in a part called TAU (Tuner and Amplifier Unit) - this has to be replaced - there are somewhere special threads about this topic.
3. Yes, it should.
4. I doubt it, but could be. Perhaps others know this for sure.

## [Tristan-cx5] 2021-12-22 (post 2525522)
Just as info: If v31 (https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Mazda+v31&tag=vs-pl-mazda3revolution-com-20&rel=nofollow) is installed, you should update to 70.00.100 (https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Mazda+firmware+update&tag=vs-pl-mazda3revolution-com-20&rel=nofollow) first, before updating to higher versions. Thats the official recommendation of Mazda (https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Mazda&tag=vs-pl-mazda3revolution-com-20&rel=nofollow).
