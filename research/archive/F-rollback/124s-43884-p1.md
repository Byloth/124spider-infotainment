## [124Scottish Abarth] 2023-11-07 (post 620159)
I think you may be confusing the CMU (entertainment centre) firmware version with the ECU firmware version. What you have above is the CMU version and if that were updated you'd have gone to v70 as v59 is old. There was not recall or update for the symptoms you describe with regards ECU software. There was a TCU (automatic transmission) update which I doubt your import folks would have access to and would have been free at a dealer as it was a safety issue that this addresses. If you have a manual transmission it wouldn't apply anyway. I would definitely ask for a detailed invoice with costs for everything they say they did as already advised and the reason for it. Ask what the root cause was (i.e. what actually caused your issue as it wasn't all 4 things). Ask which wire was shorted and ask them to show you in the car where it is. Ask them what was bad with the software, how they knew, and what source did they get the software they flashed from. (You can check the source yourself after). A wee bit of detective work and maybe then a letter from a lawyer threatening action will make them realise they added a zero to your bill and refund you.
