## [?] 2020-12-22 (post 2502393)
Yes, I tried. It`s not possible. Reason: If you have a firmware version 59.00.502 or higher installed and try to downgrade, then the lowest version shown on your usb stick will be 59.00.502 (if its on the usb stick). Lower versions will not be shown at the usb stick. So there is no way to downgrade lower than 59.00.502.
Example photos of my test:
1. Current firmware 70.00.335:
 (http://web.archive.org/web/20260107052451/https://www.mazda3revolution.com/attachments/01_current-fw-jpg.279812/)
2. Files at the usb stick: firmware 59.00.449 (so lower than 59.00.502):
 (http://web.archive.org/web/20260107052451/https://www.mazda3revolution.com/attachments/02_files-of-59-00-443-at-the-usb-stick-jpg.279813/)
3. Trying to update - but no update/downgrade files are found at the usb stick:
 (http://web.archive.org/web/20260107052451/https://www.mazda3revolution.com/attachments/03_no-update-or-downgrade-files-visible-jpg.279814/)
