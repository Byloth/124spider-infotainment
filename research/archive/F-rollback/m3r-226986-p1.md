## [williexuwf] 2018-06-28 (post 2392023)
In case someone else ran into this issue, here is what I did to fix the issue.
(a). Find an old 2GB low speed flash (https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Mazda+3+flash&tag=vs-pl-mazda3revolution-com-20&rel=nofollow) drive.
(b). Format it to FAT32 with quick format.
(c). Re-download the pkg, verify the size and hash.
The installation was completed within 20 minutes.
