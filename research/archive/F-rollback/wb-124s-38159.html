I have always used Ameridian AIO tweaks on my 2017 124 Spider and have had no issues with it. This weekend I decided to jump into adding the AA/CP mod. I have always had the ID7 patch installed since it was released, but for good measure I installed it again before I updated to the Mazda NA_70.00.100A. Installed MazdaToFiatV70AIO. Installed a few tweaks from MZD-AIO-TI to get everything looking like I did with AIO tweaks. No issues until I plugged in and ran Android Auto for the first time. No touchscreen! Didn't realize that was a side affect of doing this mod, so I played with installing AA v1.14b and just can't get it to work quite right as v70 isn't fully supported.
Followed a post (on another forum) that mentioned downgrading to 59.00.502-NA in order to properly support AA v1.14b, so I did that with no issues. Inserted the USB to run MZD-AIO-TI tweaks again and everything installed and rebooted but no tweaks! What in the world just happened? Rebooted a few more times and now it won't autorun the tweaks installer any longer off 2 USB sticks that I have been using just fine all weekend. For fun I upgraded back to NA_70.00.100A (same usb stick formatted) and still not working.
Re-read the post about downgrading and missed where they talked about installing reinstall first and failsafe second which goes again what I just did, and goes against what the manual says. Seems to be conflicting reports on how to downgrade properly, but now I am stuck on version 59.00.502 with a failsafe of 70.00.100A. The system will not recognize that I have cmu150_NA_70.00.100A_reinstall.up on the memory stick and I can't figure that out as well as why I can't run tweaks any longer even though I know I have ID7 installed as it was working just fine yesterday installing AA v1.14b.
Any thoughts or pointers would be greatly appreciated. At this point I'd rather have the tweaks than the AA, but beggars can't be choosers. Thanks guys!

@GuidoVeloce Physically swapping is easy. Our generation of CMU's are not VIN locked. I bought a Mazda 3 CMU from eBay. Phillips screwdriver to swap the mounting bracket on the bottom and I kept my screen, so it was only 4 screws to replace the screen that also has the dash part attached to it.
The only difference which I found interesting was a setting about rain sensing wipers to turn it on or off was not in the Mazda CMU version 55.XX. I tested the feature and rain sensing still worked, so I really didn't care. It wasn't until I upgraded the firmware to 59.XX then the rain sensing option came back.
