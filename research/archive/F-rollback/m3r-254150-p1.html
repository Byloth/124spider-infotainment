Hi everyone,

I’m hoping someone can help me recover from a botched MZD Connect update.

I own a Mazda6 GJ (2016), originally running version 56.00.230 EU N. I attempted an upgrade to 70.00.100A EU using the standard files from the community:

  • cmu150_EU_70.00.100A_failsafe.up
  • cmu150_EU_70.00.100A_reinstall.up

Here’s what happened:

  1. I installed the failsafe update successfully.
  2. After powering down the car (key off, battery disconnected), the CMU never came back online.
  3. I never got the chance to install the reinstall package – screen stays black.

Symptoms:

  • No display (screen completely black)
  • Radio continues playing in background
  • Commander knob non-responsive
  • No menus, no touchscreen input, no reverse camera → CMU appears bricked

Diagnostics with FORScan (OBDLink EX, latest version):

  • DTC U3000:42-0B – Control Module – General memory failure in CMU module
  • CMU is detected, but doesn’t respond to restart
  • Firmware version still shows 56.00.230 → update clearly failed

I tried rebooting the CMU via FORScan but with no success.

My Questions:

  1. Can the CMU be recovered in this condition?
    • Is there a way to trigger the reinstall update via USB, SD card, or other workaround?
  2. Would temporarily installing another CMU allow the update process to complete, then swap back?
  3. If I need a replacement:
    • Can I use a used unit from another car (same series)?
    • Does it have to be exactly the same part number?
    • What would a used CMU typically cost?

I understand this is a Mazda3 forum, but the MZD Connect system is nearly identical in the 3 and 6 models – any input would be hugely appreciated.

Thanks a lot in advance –

My ideas/answers:
1. Might be - see the thread "Black Screen MZD".
2. No, as the failed firmware install is located inside the old CMU.
3. If 1. does not work or is too complicated, a replacement would work.
  • As far as my many tests revealed, you could use any used unit from another car - also another series - e.g. CMU of a Mazda 3 for your Mazda 6. The CMU mainly reads the input it gets from other modules. And the latest new versions of the CMU have the same part no. for all models/series.
  • It does not have to be exactly the same part number.
  • Depends on the country and where you buy it - you have to check your available sources. New might be around 1000-1200 €. Used might be around 400-600 €.

Just posting for future reference for those updating with the TWO FILE 70.00.100....DO Not DO It if it is Not neccessary.

The OPs First Mistake was Installing Failsafe correctly (only takes < 5 minutes) and "powering car down", WHY?, if you are using genuine Mazda Instructions in .pdf no-where does it say to power CMU off, in fact you install failsafe.up first and leave USB Drive in Slot, immediately go back to test screen #99 and Search for 70.00.100 Reinstall.up the main file which should have already been together with the other small Fail-safe.up file and ready on your Flash Drive, Don't power anything down.

Say it again and again see my link below and do not get update files from anyone unless they can prove experience in what they are saying, UNFORTUNATELY the Sellers of New non genuine Aux Hub and Cable kits on Amazon, ALIEXPRESS, eBay etc are using old and outdated files and .txt Warning files copied from me years ago...they are totally outdated.

For the OP with 56.00.230 EU N original Firmware all your grief could have been avoided, 70.00.100 is no longer neccessary as your car is on or above 56.00.100* EU N (and 4A N), you could have just installed the far simpler 74.00.324 which is only a ONE File Update Process, far easier with less chance of going wrong, you still must watch the MAIN Warnings of.
1. Strong Battery fully charged and no older than about 4.5 years of age.
2. Pressing of Pedals BEFORE Each and Every 20 minute Period Expires, use a countdown timer will stop CMU from turning off and Bricking itself.

* File Cut Off for NA N is 55.00.650 on or Above go straight to 74.00.324 to update.

ONLY Cars with FIRMWARE BELOW or OLDER than 55.00.650 NA N like 51.00.351 for NA N, or BELOW or OLDER than 56.00.100 EU N or 4A N like 54.00.311 EU N (4A N) Must Update First with 70.00.100 (Fail-safe and Reinstall).

How You Read Firmware "Version NUMBERS" from 'Left to Right' as a 7 Digit Version Number without the 2 middle Points...

9 x Examples:
20.00.007 is 2000007 made in 2013 (late).
31.00.100A is 3100100*# made in 2014 (Only for early Mazda 3).
56.00.100 is 5600100 made in 2014 (late).
59.00.502A is 5900502 made in 2015 (late).
70.00.021 is 7000021 made in 2017.
70.00.100A is 7000100*× made in 2018.
70.00.352B is 7000352 made in 2018.
70.00.367 is 7000367 made in 2018 (late).
74.00.210A is 7400210 made in 2020.
74.00.324A is 7400324* made in late 2022 (the latest and last release).

Plus there are many Factory Versions used by Mazda in between those above during production installation, But we're never released as Standalone Update files by Mazda.

* I Only Issue what Mazda now issues, nothing else as it is all obsolete.
# Only for early Mazda 3 and is a MUST install Transition Update File, without 31 First you can NOT Update to Newer Updates.
× Only used as a Transition Update File, without 70 Next you can NOT Update to Final 74.00.324.

So with these 9 examples above 20.00.007 is the OLDEST or LOWEST buggy Version and 74.00.324A is the NEWEST or HIGHEST and the LATEST and the LAST Mazda Connect Version for this final now out of production Gen 6 Mazda-MZD Connect.

NA N or EU N or 4A N or JP M is the Region location.
NA N North America
EU N Europe/UK.
4A N (ADR) Australia/NZ, Middle East, Southern Asia, Oceania, South America, Brazil and Southern Hemisphere.

N in EU N means NNG-Here Navigation.
M in JP M means Matsuoka Japan ONLY Navigation.
