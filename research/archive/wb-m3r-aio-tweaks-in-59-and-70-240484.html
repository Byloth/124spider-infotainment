The required FW update files of .335 or .354 for NA region can be found here.

Read www.mazdatweaks.com/id7 and the ID7v2 thread for the ID7v2 process, which is necessary at .335

It is a little different from the old ID7 process of FW 59. Wiring is the same. You need to do a FW update to .335 or .354 (not .367) and at the end paste the commands (do not restart the car/mzd before pasting the commands).
