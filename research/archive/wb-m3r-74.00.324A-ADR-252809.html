To get the latest genuine Mazda MZD Connect firmware (v74.00.324A) for your Mazda 2, Mazda 3, Mazda 6, CX-3, CX-5, CX-9, or MX-5 (all vehicles using the MZD Connect system, typically 2014–2024 models):
1. Private-message @ASH8 with:
  • Your car model and year
  • Your region/country (e.g. NA N, EU N, 4A N)
  • A photo of your current firmware version screen
(Settings → System → About → Version Information)
2. New forum members must post once in any thread before private messaging.
3. Please be patient — ASH8 is based in Australia, and replies can take up to 18 hours.
📄 Full details and instructions:
🔗 Firmware Help & Request Guide

https://www.mazda3revolution.com/th...lution.com/threads/mazda-connect-firmware-update-files-genuine-are-here.236775/

⚠ Only use firmware links provided by ASH8 — files from other sites (YouTube, eBay, etc.) are often incorrect or incomplete and can brick your CMU.
This is the final official Mazda MZD Connect firmware (v74.00.324A) for the models listed above, and it includes CarPlay™ and Android Auto™ support.
MZD Connect was discontinued in newer “Gen 7” Mazdas (2019 onward) that use the newer .kwi-based Mazda Connect system.
