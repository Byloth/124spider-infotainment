Click to expand...
After installation the CMU stops sending information as it is not doing anything and is not running a full Linux system. While the CMU is displaying Reboot..... and the serial data has stopped scrolling press the PC <ENTER> key. If you do not get a # prompt or any response you have something wrong with the serial USB, transmit connection to the CMU or a settings issue in Putty. Check Putty - disconnect the transmit/receive wires from your USB adapter and short the serial adapter transmit/receive wires together, type something on the keyboard and press <ENTER> and see if the information is echoed back to the screen.



ALSO - just because the USB serial adapter transmit light blinks does not mean the data is getting into the CMU - just means the adapter is sending data out the port.
