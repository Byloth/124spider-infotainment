Official FW Download page from Mazda/Manuals/Servicing Instructions etc. - EU Only

Jump to Latest
winterarmada
For anyone that wants to download the FW for their Infotainment system you can officially download it from the Mazda Technical Download server. It can be found under https://mazdashare.com/mtds

The downloads are restricted. For that you need to register an account under the portal which is located under: https://portal.mazdaeur.com/io/index.en.html. You can register as an Independent Operator and access the locked pages. To do that and to access the restricted areas you are presented with a fee to use the portal according to the time you need the access for. Latest manuals for service and maintenance can be found for each vehicle by using your car's VIN number. We are heading at a good step regarding the EU market and the FW updates. Personally I am still looking for the JP M versions. I will keep looking.

cobra? Never heard.You want to use Android Auto via Applecare? Are you sure you know what you want and what you can expect? Does not sound like it to me...

Android Auto and Apple Carplay is included in any FW 70 and 74. But to show up/work, it requires an installed new usb hub+2 new cables. The Android Auto app is updated via your phone (and not via MZD) only.
Just FW70 or 74 (without new hardware) does not enable Android Auto or Apple Carplay.
