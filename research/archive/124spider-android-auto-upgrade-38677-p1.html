If you are trying to get just Android Auto (not Carplay) installed on your Fiat, then you really dont need any hardware upgrades. The need for the serial hack is indirectly correlated to year model, but what it is directly correlated to is your firmware version. And if your earlier SpiderCar 124 has been to a dealer, you might have newer firmware that you would expect by year model if the dealer updated your infotainment system firmware. (No idea why they might do that without your asking for it, but who knows)

IIRC firmware version 59 and up require the serial hack. Below 59 you can start with a USB stick, I believe.

My car requires the serial hack. I bought the thing that is used for the hack. That was 6+ months ago, lol. Been too busy!
